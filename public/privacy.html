Privacy Policy for Cyber Daily Feed

Cyber Daily Feed (“the app”) is a cybersecurity headline aggregator. It shows headlines, source names, and links to original publisher articles. It does not reproduce full article text.

What we store

The app stores your preferences on this device only:

  • Keyword interests you type (for example vendor or malware names)
  • Threat-category filter selections (ransomware, data breach, vulnerability/patch, nation-state, phishing)

These values are kept in local on-device storage. They are not sent to a Cyber Daily Feed server. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no advertising identifier collection by this app.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or contact book access
  • No precise location
  • No server-side personal data store
  • No sale of personal information

Network requests

To refresh headlines, the app requests public RSS (and, if needed, public listing) feeds from third-party publishers: Dark Reading, The Hacker News, Bleeping Computer, Krebs on Security, and CISA. Those publishers receive standard request metadata from your device (IP address, user agent) under their own policies. The app does not attach your keyword list or tag selections to those requests.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
